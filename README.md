# Study Planner
A study planner app that lives entirely in your browser, letting you map sessions across the week, drag cards when priorities shift, and see where prep time is piling up.

![study-planner-v1](study-planner-v1.png)

## Designed for
- Students juggling multiple exams in one week
- Spreading review sessions across available days
- Tracking daily prep time at a glance
- Rearranging blocks when priorities shift

## Features & capabilities
- Quick-add event sheet: Click any day column to open a slide-in form with title, duration chips, optional notes, and a test toggle that saves on Enter.
- Duration chip picker: Select from preset chips for 15, 20, 30, 45, or 60 minutes, or type a custom number for sessions of any length.
- Cross-column drag: Drag a card from one day to another to reschedule it, with both columns updating their totals the moment you drop.
- Within-day reorder: Drag cards up or down inside a single column to set the sequence you want, saved automatically to local storage.
- Test flag toggle: Mark a card as the test itself so it stands out with an amber badge and its duration stays out of your study total.
- Week navigation: Step backward or forward by week with arrow buttons, or jump straight to today, to lay out sessions weeks in advance.
- Local storage persistence: Your entire plan saves to the browser automatically and survives page refreshes, with no sign-up or account required.
- Clear and restart: Wipe every event across all weeks with a single confirmed action, returning the app to its welcome screen for a fresh start.

## About Study Planner
This study planner app gives you a drag-and-drop weekly grid where you map out every review session and practice set alongside each test day in one view. Open it in your browser, click a day, add a block, and see your prep time spread across the week.

These application has seven-column day grid with stacked event cards, a quick-add sheet with duration chips, drag-and-drop reordering powered by DnD Kit, and per-day time totals that update the moment you move a card. Add subjects through the quick-add sheet, then arrange their cards on the weekly grid.

Everything runs on a single page. The week heading with arrow navigation lets you look ahead or review past weeks. Each event card shows a title and a duration badge. Test dates can also carry a flag. A toolbar gives you clipboard export, a print-ready layout, and a one-click reset.

Compared to a generic calendar or a hand-drawn spreadsheet, this template puts the shape of your week front and center. You see at a glance which days are heavy and which have room for one more session.
